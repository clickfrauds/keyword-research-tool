Strategy Notes

Strategy silos users by repair site (general detection vs underground/main line vs slab vs generic pipe vs household fixtures) so no two ad groups compete for the same query, with emergency/urgent intent isolated for premium bidding. Excluded IDs are consumer smart-sensor product searches, competitor brand names, gas-leak/pool/irrigation queries, and DIY/informational terms irrelevant to a Dubai leak detection and repair service business. Budget and bids favor core detection, pipe repair and slab repair (highest volume/CPC, strongest transactional intent) while fixture repair and system installation run at standard-to-lower multipliers as supporting revenue lines.
